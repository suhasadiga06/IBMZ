Read this first

The labels mark sessions from batteries the dataset authors identified as faulty. They are not fire records, and no link to fires is documented. ChargeGuard is evaluated as early detection of potentially hazardous charging anomalies; it does not predict or prevent fires.

Each level is entered when the score stays at or above its threshold for 2 consecutive readings, never before minute 3.
