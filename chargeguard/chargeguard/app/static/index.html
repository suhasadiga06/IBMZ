      ChargeGuard is a datathon prototype. It flags charging sessions whose readings look unusual next to normal charges; it does not diagnose batteries and does not predict or prevent fires. The labels it learned from mark sessions from faulty batteries, not fire outcomes. Driver IDs are pseudonymised (HMAC) before storage. How well it works, and where it does not →
